# Lumos Connect

LinkedIn outreach from your own computer, for Mac and Windows. Free.

## Download

Go to **[the latest release](https://github.com/DaveMatiska/lumos-connect-releases/releases/latest)** and download:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4…) | `LumosConnect-…-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel chip | `LumosConnect-…-mac-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `LumosConnect-…-Setup.exe` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip" or "Processor".

## Installing (one-time extra step)

These first versions aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer warns you once:

- **Mac:** open the .dmg and drag Lumos Connect into Applications. In Applications, **right-click** Lumos Connect → **Open** → **Open**. (Newer macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → **Open Anyway**.)
- **Windows:** when "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

After that, Lumos Connect updates itself automatically. Every update is digitally signed and checked before it's used.

## Important

LinkedIn's rules don't allow automation, so your LinkedIn account could be restricted. You use Lumos Connect at your own risk; the app shows the full terms of use when you first open it. Your data (settings, messages, contacts, LinkedIn login) stays on your computer.

The other files in each release (`app-*.zip`, `latest.json`, `latest.json.sig`) are the automatic update. You don't need to download them.
